Shift Ledger
Privacy Policy

Effective: 31 October 2026 · Advertising and subscriptions
Application ID: com.kedge.shiftledger

Shift planning, time tracking, pay estimates and reports work offline. Your work records stay in the app's private storage. The free version includes advertising, subject to age confirmation and applicable privacy choices. An optional monthly subscription removes ads. Privacy choices do not restrict offline ledger tools.

1. Publisher and contact

Shift Ledger is published by kedge, Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. For privacy questions or support, email kedgeyuan@gmail.com. Please avoid sending complete payslips, identification documents or unnecessary financial details.

2. Your work records

The app stores job names, sites entered by you, shift schedules, actual work times, breaks, hourly rates, overtime settings, pay adjustments, reconciliation amounts, notes, preferences and timer timestamps on your device. These are used to perform the functions you request. We operate no ledger server or app account and do not upload these records to the publisher, InMobi or a consent service. A site name is text you enter; the app does not request GPS or other Android location permissions.

3. Advertising and consent services

Version 1.2.0 includes the InMobi advertising SDK and InMobi CMP. They are initialized only after you confirm you are at least 18. Advertising also requires an internet connection, valid publisher configuration and applicable consent. Where consent cannot be established or advertising is unavailable, the offline tools continue to work.

When these online services run, InMobi and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device/OS/app details, language and network information, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information. CMP processes consent selections and device/network information to obtain and store privacy choices. Processing supports advertising, measurement, security and consent management. We do not supply your wages, job names, sites, notes, shift history or backup files to either SDK.

See InMobi's privacy policy and its linked regional notices for recipients, retention, international processing, rights and contact details. We do not control third-party retention. Ad destinations you open have their own policies.

4. Advertising choices and subscriptions

The free version includes ads. Settings → Privacy choices lets you revisit available regional consent choices. Display language or country never acts as legal consent. Android advertising ID controls are also available in system settings. Ads are not requested while a verified ad-free subscription is active. An SDK already started may retain its current session; close and reopen the app to end that session.

Google Play handles subscription payments and billing information under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive your card details. To verify and restore ad-free access, the app sends a purchase token to our verification service at apps.ai3737.com, which checks the purchase state, product and expiry with the Google Play Developer API and acknowledges successful purchases. The service does not receive work records or ledger exports and processes purchase tokens in memory without storing them in a database or logging request bodies. HTTP access logging is disabled for the verification endpoint. Hosting infrastructure may process IP addresses and operational metadata as needed to deliver requests and operate the service. The app privately caches verification and expiry timestamps for no more than seven days and never beyond the paid period. Clearing app data removes that cache; Google Play remains responsible for its own purchase history retention. To cancel renewal, use Google Play's subscription management; uninstalling the app does not cancel billing.

5. Exports, backups and links

CSV, PDF and JSON exports are created only when you request them. You select their destination through Android's document picker. Files can contain personal financial information; JSON backups are readable and are not encrypted by this app. A selected cloud document provider may upload them under its own terms. Choose local storage to keep exports offline. Opening this policy or an external link uses your browser and that website's connection; GitHub Pages hosts this policy under GitHub's privacy statement.

6. Notifications and home widget

Optional local reminders and the home widget can display job names and shift times on your device. Android notification permission can be refused or revoked without disabling the ledger. Remove the widget or disable notifications if you do not want those details visible.

7. Retention, deletion and security

Work records remain until you erase the ledger in the app, clear app storage or uninstall. Erasing the ledger also deletes its pre-import recovery copy; advertising preferences are separate and can be changed in Settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-owned data. Previously exported files remain at your chosen destination and must be deleted separately. Android automatic backup and device transfer of the app's data are disabled, so make a manual backup before uninstalling or moving phones.

Android's application sandbox protects private app storage. The app does not provide database encryption, an app PIN, encrypted exports or remote recovery. Protect your device and exported files. We cannot retrieve a ledger that was never sent to us.

8. Children and privacy requests

This is a work-record tool for adult shift workers and is not directed to children. Advertising is unavailable unless the user confirms they are at least 18. If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond and resolve the request; you may request deletion of that correspondence. Rights concerning data processed by InMobi can be exercised using the mechanisms in its privacy policy. Local ledger deletion is available directly on your device.

9. Changes

Updates to data practices will be reflected here and, where necessary, in the app and Google Play disclosures. Material changes requiring a new choice will be presented before the relevant processing starts.
